What Storyfield is

Storyfield is a free listening app. New narrators read classic books aloud, with commentary along the way. Every recording builds the narrator's public portfolio, so listeners discover voices before anyone else does.

Listening without an account

You can listen straight away. Your place in each book and your downloads are saved on your phone. A free account lets you follow narrators, get notified when they publish, narrate, and keep your listening on any phone.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings → Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. That removes your profile, follows, listening history, and anything you uploaded. If you listened without an account, deleting the app removes what was stored on your phone. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Reporting content

Use Report on a reading or a narrator's page, or email us with a link or the narrator's handle. You can also block a narrator from their page.
